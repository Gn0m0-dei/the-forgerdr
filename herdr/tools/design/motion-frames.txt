MOTION FRAMES (forgerdr's own, after the forgnome design)
=========================================================

The frames of the signs in design 11's motion table. The design draws one frame of each sign (the state glyphs in
golem-parts.txt and the zZ glyph in gnome-parts.txt); the other frames are drawn here for the forgerdr mod. Every sign
is 8x8. A letter is one colour layer of the sign, a dot is empty; each layer is one character of the Forgerdr Glyphs
font, which herdr/tools/glyphs.mts makes from this file, so the mod draws a layer as text in its colour and steps it
through the frames with .frames() (herdr/mods/forgerdr/kit/motion.js has the timing and the colours).

A sign starts with "== <name>", a frame with "-- <number>"; anything else is a note.
