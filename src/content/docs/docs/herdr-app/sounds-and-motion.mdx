## Motion

The look moves as the design's motion table says, in steps and never eased:

- a **flame** beside the gnome of a forge at work;
- a **`!`** that blinks beside it and on its tab while a golem waits on you;
- a **spark** when one is done;
- a rising **zZ** over a forge with no golem, and over the empty state.

Each sign is a font of frames that the app steps natively, so nothing runs while the window is not drawn. `herdr-app do forgerdr.motion`, or a key bound to `forgerdr.motion` in `keys.json`, turns the motion off and on again.

## Sounds

The app's alerts sound like the forge with `bin/forge-herdr-install.sh --sounds` ([setup](../../getting-started/setup/) offers it):

| When | The sound |
|---|---|
| A golem needs you | the hammer working the piece on the anvil |
| A golem is done | the hiss of a quench |
| A golem cracks on an API error | its stone splitting, played by the mod while the app's sounds are on |

A sound of your own is kept, and `--remove` takes them away. The three are synthesised by a script in the repository, so they carry no one else's licence.

## Notifications

Notifications, the app's own and every mod's, are the forge's too: the gnome of the forge they are about tells them, in the colour and mood of their level, coming in three steps and never taking the keyboard. A click goes where they point.
